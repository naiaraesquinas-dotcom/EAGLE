\section{Data Source Descriptions}

\subsection{REE -- REData}

\begin{description}

    \item[\textbf{Source:}]
    Red Eléctrica de España (Redeia).

    \item[\textbf{Access:}]
    \url{https://www.ree.es/es/datos/apidatos}

    \item[\textbf{Description:}]
    Official data platform of the Spanish electricity system providing
    historical and operational information through a REST API.

    \item[\textbf{Relevant data:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Electricity demand.
        \item Generation by technology.
        \item Generation mix.
        \item Installed generation capacity.
        \item Renewable generation and capacity.
        \item Electricity balance.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Spatial coverage:}]
    Spanish electricity system, including geographical disaggregation
    for different electrical systems and regions depending on the dataset.

    \item[\textbf{Temporal coverage and resolution:}]
    Historical time series with hourly, daily, monthly or annual
    aggregation depending on the dataset.

    \item[\textbf{Data format:}]
    JSON through the API. Some datasets can also be downloaded
    through the web interface.

    \item[\textbf{Access method:}]
    REST API and REData web interface.

    \item[\textbf{Processing required:}]
    API queries, JSON parsing, temporal filtering, data cleaning and
    transformation into tabular time series suitable for the EAGLE model.

    \item[\textbf{Main limitations:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Does not provide a detailed electrical topology of the
        Spanish transmission network.
        \item Does not provide the electrical parameters required to
        model individual transmission lines.
        \item Most information is aggregated rather than nodal.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Application in EAGLE:}]
    Construction of realistic historical demand and generation time
    series and validation of operational and future scenarios simulated
    with the EAGLE model.

\end{description}

\subsection{CNMC -- Electricity Network Access Capacity Maps}

\begin{description}

    \item[\textbf{Source:}]
    Comisión Nacional de los Mercados y la Competencia (CNMC).

    \item[\textbf{Access:}]
    \url{https://www.cnmc.es/prensa/mapas-capacidad-redes-electricas-20260415}

    \item[\textbf{Description:}]
    Official CNMC platform integrating information on available electricity
    network access capacity published by Spanish transmission and distribution
    system operators. Separate interactive maps are available for generation
    and demand access capacity.

    \item[\textbf{Relevant data:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Electricity network nodes and substations.
        \item Available generation access capacity.
        \item Available demand access capacity.
        \item Transmission or distribution system operator.
        \item Province.
        \item Voltage level.
        \item Number of available positions at substations.
        \item Generation access capacity differentiated by technology
        for the transmission network.
        \item Demand access capacity according to the applicable
        evaluation criteria.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Spatial coverage:}]
    Spanish electricity transmission and distribution networks.

    \item[\textbf{Temporal coverage and resolution:}]
    Current network access-capacity information updated monthly by the
    corresponding transmission and distribution system operators.

    \item[\textbf{Data format:}]
    Interactive geospatial maps integrating information supplied by the
    Spanish electricity network operators. The underlying publication
    formats depend on the corresponding network operator.

    \item[\textbf{Access method:}]
    CNMC interactive generation and demand access-capacity maps. The
    original information can also be consulted through the corresponding
    transmission and distribution system operators.

    \item[\textbf{Processing required:}]
    Identification of relevant nodes and voltage levels, extraction of
    access-capacity information and geographical/electrical matching with
    the nodes of the EAGLE network model.

    \item[\textbf{Main limitations:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Access capacity is not equivalent to the thermal capacity
        or rating of a transmission line.
        \item The platform does not constitute a complete electrical
        network model.
        \item It does not provide the complete set of line impedance
        parameters required for power-flow simulation.
        \item Data originate from multiple transmission and distribution
        system operators and may require additional harmonisation.
        \item Available access capacities should not necessarily be treated
        as simultaneously available or directly additive between nodes.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Application in EAGLE:}]
    Provides an official and frequently updated reference for the available
    generation and demand access capacity at Spanish electricity network
    nodes. Particularly relevant for validating or complementing the
    hosting-capacity indicators calculated by EAGLE and for identifying
    areas where new electricity demand may face connection constraints.

\end{description}

\subsection{PyPSA-Eur}

\begin{description}

    \item[\textbf{Source:}]
    PyPSA-Eur project, currently maintained by the Department of Digital
    Transformation in Energy Systems at the Technical University of Berlin.

    \item[\textbf{Access:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}

        \item Official PyPSA-Eur repository:
        \url{https://github.com/PyPSA/pypsa-eur}

        \item Latest PyPSA-Eur releases:
        \url{https://github.com/PyPSA/pypsa-eur/releases}

        \item Official PyPSA-Eur documentation:
        \url{https://pypsa-eur.readthedocs.io/en/latest/}

        \item Data sources and data versioning:
        \url{https://pypsa-eur.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data_sources/}

        \item PyPSA public data repository:
        \url{https://data.pypsa.org/}

    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Description:}]
    Open model dataset of the European energy system at transmission-network
    level. PyPSA-Eur combines multiple open data sources through a reproducible
    Snakemake workflow and produces a simulation-ready European electricity
    network compatible with the PyPSA modelling framework.

    \item[\textbf{Relevant data:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item High-voltage electricity transmission network.
        \item Network buses and substations.
        \item AC transmission lines and cables.
        \item HVDC links.
        \item Geographical coordinates and network geometry.
        \item Voltage levels.
        \item Electrical line parameters.
        \item Transmission capacities.
        \item Existing power plants, including location, capacity and
        technology.
        \item Electricity demand time series.
        \item Spatial distribution of electricity demand.
        \item Renewable generation availability time series.
        \item Geographical wind and solar generation potentials.
        \item Additional energy-system data used for sector-coupled
        modelling.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Spatial coverage:}]
    European energy system covering the ENTSO-E area, including Spain.
    The default electricity network represents the high-voltage
    transmission system, while lower voltage thresholds can optionally
    be considered when constructing the network.

    \item[\textbf{Temporal coverage and resolution:}]
    Depends on the individual dataset used by the PyPSA-Eur workflow.
    The model supports hourly time-series representation and combines
    network data with historical electricity demand, renewable-resource
    and energy-system datasets. Input datasets are independently
    versioned to ensure reproducibility.

    \item[\textbf{Data format:}]
    Multiple machine-readable formats are used throughout the workflow,
    including CSV, GeoJSON and NetCDF. The final electricity network can
    be stored as a PyPSA network in NetCDF format.

    \item[\textbf{Access method:}]
    Open-source PyPSA-Eur repository and automated data-retrieval workflow.
    Input datasets can be retrieved automatically according to the versions
    defined by PyPSA-Eur. Publicly redistributable datasets are also mirrored
    through the PyPSA data infrastructure.

    \item[\textbf{Processing required:}]
    Configuration of the PyPSA-Eur workflow, selection of Spain as the
    geographical scope, retrieval of the required input datasets and
    construction of the electricity network. Additional validation and
    replacement of estimated parameters with official Spanish data should
    be performed where higher accuracy is required.

    \item[\textbf{Main limitations:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item The transmission network is primarily reconstructed from
        OpenStreetMap rather than obtained directly from an official
        Spanish TSO electrical model.
        \item Some electrical network parameters and capacities are derived
        or estimated rather than obtained directly from official TSO data.
        \item Completeness and accuracy therefore depend partly on the
        underlying OpenStreetMap information and reconstruction procedure.
        \item Different input datasets have different reference years,
        licences and update frequencies.
        \item The European model may require Spanish-specific validation
        and enrichment for detailed nodal hosting-capacity studies.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Application in EAGLE:}]
    Strong candidate for the initial electrical-network dataset of the
    EAGLE model. PyPSA-Eur can provide a geographically referenced and
    simulation-ready representation of the Spanish high-voltage transmission
    network together with generation, demand and renewable-resource
    information. The Spanish subset can be used as the initial EAGLE network
    and subsequently validated, corrected and enriched with official Spanish
    sources such as REData, CNMC and Red Eléctrica.

\end{description}

\subsection{REE -- Transmission Network Access Capacity}

\begin{description}

    \item[\textbf{Source:}]
    Red Eléctrica de España (Redeia), as Spanish Transmission System
    Operator (TSO).

    \item[\textbf{Access:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}

        \item Demand access capacity:
        \url{https://www.ree.es/es/clientes/consumidor/acceso-conexion/conoce-la-capacidad-de-acceso}

        \item Generation access capacity:
        \url{https://www.ree.es/es/clientes/generador/acceso-conexion/conoce-la-capacidad-de-acceso}

        \item Status of demand access requests:
        \url{https://www.ree.es/es/clientes/consumidor/acceso-conexion/conoce-el-estado-de-las-solicitudes}

    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Description:}]
    Official information published by Red Eléctrica on the available and
    occupied access capacity at nodes of the Spanish transmission network.
    Separate datasets are published for electricity demand and generation.
    The information provides a direct reference for evaluating the capability
    of transmission-network nodes to accommodate additional consumption or
    generation.

    \item[\textbf{Relevant data:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Transmission-network nodes.
        \item Voltage level of the connection node.
        \item Available demand access capacity.
        \item Occupied demand access capacity.
        \item Available generation access capacity.
        \item Occupied generation access capacity.
        \item Capacity associated with existing access permissions.
        \item Capacity reserved for demand connected through distribution
        networks.
        \item Capacity associated with storage where applicable.
        \item Capacity-sharing zones between different substations.
        \item Information on dedicated positions for demand.
        \item Nodes subject to specific access-capacity conditions or
        allocation procedures.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Spatial coverage:}]
    Nodes of the Spanish electricity transmission network managed by
    Red Eléctrica. The datasets provide node-level information and are
    therefore considerably more spatially detailed than national or
    regional aggregated electricity-system statistics.

    \item[\textbf{Temporal coverage and resolution:}]
    Current access-capacity information rather than historical electrical
    operating time series. The published access capacities are periodically
    updated by Red Eléctrica. At the time of this assessment, the available
    demand and generation capacity datasets are updated monthly.

    \item[\textbf{Data format:}]
    Access-capacity results are published in machine-readable and
    human-readable formats, including CSV, XLSX and PDF. Additional
    documentation describing the calculation criteria and interpretation
    of the published fields is also available.

    \item[\textbf{Access method:}]
    Direct download from the Red Eléctrica access-and-connection website.
    CSV and XLSX files can be processed automatically and linked with the
    corresponding transmission-network nodes of the EAGLE model.

    \item[\textbf{Processing required:}]
    Download and parsing of the published files, identification of the
    corresponding transmission-network nodes, normalisation of node names
    and voltage levels, and geographical/electrical matching with the buses
    of the EAGLE network model. Particular attention should be given to the
    definitions of available, occupied and reserved access capacity.

    \item[\textbf{Main limitations:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Access capacity is not equivalent to the thermal rating of
        an individual transmission line.

        \item Published capacity represents the result of an access-capacity
        assessment considering several network criteria and should not be
        interpreted as a simple physical line limit.

        \item Available capacity values at different nodes should not
        automatically be assumed to be simultaneously available or directly
        additive.

        \item The published information does not constitute a complete
        electrical transmission-network model.

        \item Detailed line impedance parameters required for independent
        power-flow calculations are not provided through this dataset.

        \item Published values have an informative character and do not
        replace the specific access study performed for an individual
        connection request.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Application in EAGLE:}]
    This dataset is particularly relevant for EAGLE because the project
    aims to evaluate the capability of the electricity network to accommodate
    new large electricity consumers such as data centres. The official
    demand access capacity published for transmission-network nodes can be
    geographically and electrically matched with the corresponding buses
    of the EAGLE model.

    It can therefore be used both as a direct grid-availability indicator
    for SiteOptimizer and as an official reference for validating the nodal
    hosting-capacity indicators calculated independently using the EAGLE
    electrical-network model.

\end{description}

\subsection{ENTSO-E -- Transparency Platform and Grid Data}

\begin{description}

    \item[\textbf{Source:}]
    European Network of Transmission System Operators for Electricity
    (ENTSO-E).

    \item[\textbf{Access:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}

        \item ENTSO-E Transparency Platform:
        \url{https://transparency.entsoe.eu/}

        \item Transparency Platform information:
        \url{https://www.entsoe.eu/data/transparency-platform/}

        \item ENTSO-E statistics and grid datasets:
        \url{https://www.entsoe.eu/publications/statistics-and-data/}

        \item CIM / CGMES grid-model information:
        \url{https://www.entsoe.eu/data/cim/cim-for-grid-models-exchange/}

    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Description:}]
    ENTSO-E provides harmonised European electricity-system information
    through several platforms and datasets. The Transparency Platform
    publishes operational and market-related information supplied by
    European transmission system operators, while additional ENTSO-E
    resources provide transmission-network datasets and standardised
    Common Information Model (CIM) / Common Grid Model Exchange
    Specification (CGMES) information.

    \item[\textbf{Relevant data:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Actual electricity load.
        \item Day-ahead and longer-term load forecasts.
        \item Actual generation.
        \item Generation by production type.
        \item Installed generation capacity.
        \item Wind and solar generation forecasts.
        \item Cross-border electricity flows.
        \item Transmission information.
        \item Transmission and generation outages.
        \item Balancing information.
        \item European bidding-zone and system-level information.
        \item European transmission-grid models for specific studies,
        subject to dataset-specific access conditions.
        \item CIM / CGMES specifications and reference information for
        exchanging detailed transmission-network models.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Spatial coverage:}]
    European electricity system covering the ENTSO-E area, including
    Spain and its electrical interconnections with neighbouring systems.
    The spatial resolution depends strongly on the specific dataset:
    Transparency Platform information is commonly provided at bidding-zone
    or system level, whereas dedicated grid-model datasets can contain
    considerably more detailed transmission-network information.

    \item[\textbf{Temporal coverage and resolution:}]
    Depends on the dataset. The Transparency Platform provides historical,
    current and forecast electricity-system information with resolutions
    appropriate to the corresponding load, generation, transmission,
    balancing or outage dataset.

    \item[\textbf{Data format:}]
    Transparency Platform information can be downloaded in machine-readable
    formats including XML, CSV and XLSX depending on the access method and
    dataset. Detailed grid models are increasingly exchanged using CIM /
    CGMES-based representations.

    \item[\textbf{Access method:}]
    Data can be consulted and downloaded through the ENTSO-E Transparency
    Platform. Programmatic access is available through the ENTSO-E
    Transparency Platform REST API for supported datasets.

    Detailed grid-model datasets have different access conditions.
    Some datasets are available to universities, companies and other
    institutions upon request, while access to more advanced network
    datasets may require additional agreements or a Non-Disclosure
    Agreement.

    \item[\textbf{Processing required:}]
    Selection of the relevant Spanish bidding zone or network dataset,
    automated data retrieval where appropriate, parsing of the corresponding
    XML/CSV/XLSX or CGMES representation, temporal harmonisation and
    conversion into the internal data structures used by the EAGLE
    electrical model.

    \item[\textbf{Main limitations:}] \hfill
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Transparency Platform data should not be interpreted as a
        directly downloadable complete electrical model of the Spanish
        transmission network.

        \item Much of the operational information is aggregated at
        bidding-zone or system level rather than individual electrical
        nodes.

        \item Detailed European grid datasets have dataset-specific access
        conditions and are not necessarily fully open for unrestricted
        download.

        \item Access to some advanced network datasets may require a formal
        request and a Non-Disclosure Agreement.

        \item The spatial and temporal resolution varies considerably
        between datasets.

        \item CGMES models require specialised processing before they can
        be directly used in the selected EAGLE simulation framework.
    \end{itemize}

    \item[\textbf{Application in EAGLE:}]
    ENTSO-E can provide an independent European reference for validating
    Spanish demand, generation, renewable production and cross-border
    electricity exchanges. This is particularly useful for validating the
    boundary conditions of the Spanish EAGLE model and its electrical
    interconnections with neighbouring countries.

    ENTSO-E is also relevant as a potential source of more detailed
    transmission-network models in CIM/CGMES format if suitable datasets
    can be obtained. Such models could be compared with the reconstructed
    PyPSA-Eur Spanish network and used to validate or improve its topology
    and electrical parameters.

\end{description}
